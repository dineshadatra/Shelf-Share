# Legal Review Checklist — ShelfShare
## Lawyer, CA, CS and Payment Provider Sign-off

**Research cut-off:** 30 October 2026  
**Status:** Checklist only. No item is deemed approved or completed.  
**Legend:** `OPEN` = not approved; `EVIDENCE REQUIRED` = attach signed advice, registration, provider confirmation or filing proof; `N/A` = professional must record reason.

---

## A. Company and governance — CS / lawyer

- [ ] Confirm ShelfShare is an e-commerce entity / marketplace e-commerce entity under Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
- [ ] Confirm Rule 4(1)(a) company incorporation requirement and select Indian Private Limited Company structure.
- [ ] Approve company name after MCA and trademark checks.
- [ ] Finalise founders, shareholding, director appointments, registered office and board governance.
- [ ] Review MOA objects for marketplace, technology, rental facilitation and related lawful business activities.
- [ ] File SPICe+ Part A/Part B and linked MCA V3 forms.
- [ ] Obtain Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, TAN, DIN/DSC and statutory registers.
- [ ] Review shareholder/founder agreements, IP assignment, confidentiality and employee/contractor agreements.
- [ ] Appoint resident nodal contact / senior designated functionary for E-Commerce Rules.
- [ ] Appoint consumer grievance officer and publish name/designation/contact details.
- [ ] Determine corporate annual filings, board meetings, statutory audit and accounting calendar.
- [ ] Confirm any DPIIT Startup recognition decision separately; do not treat it as a substitute for incorporation or compliance.

**Evidence:** CS sign-off, incorporation certificate, MCA filings, MOA/AOA, board resolutions, governance calendar.

## B. E-Commerce Rules and consumer law — lawyer

- [ ] Confirm marketplace classification and all obligations under Rules 2, 4, 5 and 6.
- [ ] Review reader terms, owner/seller terms, rental agreement and platform terms.
- [ ] Confirm required seller/owner information disclosures and how they reconcile with user privacy.
- [ ] Confirm owner undertaking for accurate listing, photographs, condition and pricing.
- [ ] Confirm written contract with each owner/seller.
- [ ] Confirm total-price disclosure, itemised fees, tax, deposit, cancellation and refund terms.
- [ ] Confirm ranking-parameter disclosure and treatment of paid/sponsored listings.
- [ ] Confirm differentiated treatment disclosures in seller terms.
- [ ] Confirm seller and platform grievance contacts and complaint ticket requirements.
- [ ] Confirm consumer cancellation charge symmetry under Rule 4(8).
- [ ] Confirm explicit affirmative consent at checkout; remove pre-ticked boxes.
- [ ] Confirm refund execution timelines and handling of failed refunds.
- [ ] Review CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines, 2023 against every checkout, onboarding, subscription and cancellation screen.
- [ ] Create annual dark-pattern self-audit and certificate process for 2027 amendment.
- [ ] Review National Consumer Helpline convergence/partnership onboarding.
- [ ] Prepare 2027 complaint-copy and NCH workflows.
- [ ] Review price-history and 30-day prior-price logic before promotions.
- [ ] Obtain and review full Gazette G.S.R. 789(E), 9 September 2026, for 1 January 2027 amendment.

**Evidence:** Written legal memo, final policies, UI sign-off, grievance SOP, dark-pattern audit report, NCH onboarding proof.

## C. GST — CA

- [ ] Confirm GST registration as e-commerce operator under CGST Act section 24(x), including states and timing.
- [ ] Confirm whether platform's owner/reader arrangement is a taxable supply of rental services.
- [ ] Determine SAC/HSN and GST rate for physical-book rental.
- [ ] Determine whether any public-library lending exemption is available; do not assume it applies to private P2P rental.
- [ ] Confirm tax on ShelfShare's 10% commission/platform fee.
- [ ] Confirm tax on convenience fee, delivery, late fee, damage recovery, cancellation charge and other amounts.
- [ ] Confirm security deposit treatment at collection and at any later appropriation/forfeiture.
- [ ] Determine owner registration requirements and applicable small-supplier exceptions/notifications.
- [ ] Determine whether owners are registered suppliers for section 52 TCS purposes.
- [ ] Confirm current section 52 TCS rate: intra-State 0.25% CGST + 0.25% SGST/UTGST; inter-State 0.5% IGST, effective 10 July 2024.
- [ ] Confirm net taxable supply calculation, returns/credit notes and monthly TCS reporting.
- [ ] Confirm GSTR-8 and related return/payment deadlines.
- [ ] Confirm invoices/receipts for owner rental supply and platform fee.
- [ ] Confirm input tax credit eligibility and allocation.
- [ ] Confirm place-of-supply and inter-/intra-State treatment for Hyderabad/Bengaluru transactions.
- [ ] Reconcile PA settlement reports, platform ledger, GST returns and refunds.

**Evidence:** CA GST opinion, GSTIN(s), tax matrix, approved invoice formats, GSTR-8 process, reconciliation test.

## D. Income-tax withholding — CA

- [ ] Confirm Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to post-1 April 2026 credits/payments.
- [ ] Review section 393(1), Table Sl. No. 8(v) for 0.1% TDS on gross amount paid/credited to e-commerce participants.
- [ ] Confirm which owner types qualify as e-commerce participants.
- [ ] Confirm section 393(4), Table Sl. No. 11 exception for individual/HUF participants with annual gross amount not exceeding ₹5 lakh and PAN/Aadhaar furnished.
- [ ] Confirm gross base and treatment of platform commission, GST, deposit, refunds and chargebacks.
- [ ] Confirm time of deduction (credit/payment, whichever earlier).
- [ ] Obtain and validate PAN/Aadhaar in a lawful, minimised manner.
- [ ] Obtain TAN and configure TDS workflows where required.
- [ ] Confirm deposit, statement, certificate, correction and record-keeping obligations under the new Act/rules.
- [ ] Ensure post-1 April 2026 filings use new section/table references, not old section 194-O.
- [ ] Reconcile owner gross amounts, withholding, payout and certificates.

**Evidence:** CA withholding memo, owner tax classification, sample TDS calculation, return calendar and test reconciliation.

## E. Payments and deposits — lawyer + CA + payment provider

- [ ] Confirm selected PA is currently RBI-authorised for relevant domestic payment activity.
- [ ] Confirm product is explicitly approved for marketplace / multi-vendor P2P rentals.
- [ ] Confirm owner sub-merchant onboarding and KYC.
- [ ] Confirm split settlement and platform commission deduction.
- [ ] Confirm settlement trigger and any hold/retention window is permitted by RBI rules and provider contract.
- [ ] Confirm refund, partial refund, failed refund, chargeback and reversal support.
- [ ] Confirm provider's treatment of deposits and whether it can lawfully retain them pending return/dispute.
- [ ] Obtain written confirmation of deposit legal account structure and beneficial ownership.
- [ ] Confirm funds are not pooled in ShelfShare's ordinary operating account for onward merchant payout.
- [ ] Confirm no unlicensed wallet, stored value, PPI or informal escrow is created.
- [ ] Confirm no platform database balance is represented as legally held money.
- [ ] Confirm owner payout destination verification and payout failure handling.
- [ ] Confirm reconciliation and audit reports.
- [ ] Confirm fees, reserves, rolling holds, prohibited categories and dispute timelines.
- [ ] Confirm provider contract covers marketplace roles and consumer disclosures.

**Evidence:** RBI authorisation verification, signed provider agreement, provider product approval, legal opinion, payment flow diagram, test environment evidence.

## F. Security deposit policy — lawyer + payment provider

- [ ] Decide whether a deposit is required for every book, selected categories or selected copy values.
- [ ] Decide lawful collection/authorisation mechanism.
- [ ] Identify entity legally holding funds and account structure.
- [ ] Define when deposit becomes refundable.
- [ ] Define how a damage claim pauses or affects release.
- [ ] Define partial release of undisputed amount, if allowed.
- [ ] Define maximum claim, evidence, decision maker and appeal.
- [ ] Define treatment if book is not returned.
- [ ] Define failed deposit refund and chargeback.
- [ ] Ensure no automatic confiscation.
- [ ] Ensure deposit is not counted as rent, platform revenue or a fictional wallet balance.
- [ ] Confirm accounting and tax treatment.

**Evidence:** Signed legal opinion, provider confirmation, approved Deposit Policy, ledger and reconciliation design.

## G. DPDP privacy and data governance — lawyer / privacy specialist / security lead

- [ ] Map personal data and processing purposes for account, OTP, profile photo, identity documents, location, payments, chat, evidence, reviews and fraud controls.
- [ ] Identify ShelfShare as Data Fiduciary and classify each provider as Data Processor or independent fiduciary as applicable.
- [ ] Prepare standalone, clear privacy notice with itemised data and purposes.
- [ ] Confirm lawful basis and consent flow for each processing purpose.
- [ ] Separate mandatory service processing from optional promotional consent.
- [ ] Make consent withdrawal as easy as giving consent where consent is used.
- [ ] Implement Data Principal rights request intake and grievance process.
- [ ] Determine child/under-18 exclusion and age-verification method.
- [ ] Review restrictions on tracking/behavioural monitoring/targeted advertising for children.
- [ ] Execute processor agreements with hosting, SMS, identity and analytics providers.
- [ ] Approve access controls and least privilege for identity evidence.
- [ ] Approve retention/deletion schedule by data type.
- [ ] Approve breach response and statutory notification playbook.
- [ ] Confirm whether Rule 4 commencement on 13 Nov 2026 creates immediate additional tasks for ShelfShare.
- [ ] Prepare for substantive DPDP Act and Rules commencement on 13 May 2027.
- [ ] Assess whether ShelfShare could be designated a Significant Data Fiduciary as scale evolves.

**Evidence:** Data inventory, privacy notice, consent records, processor agreements, retention schedule, DPIA/risk assessment if advised, breach runbook, rights workflow.

- [ ] Register headers.
- [ ] Register content templates for OTP, transactional and service messages.
- [ ] Register consent templates where applicable.
- [ ] Confirm message classification and user preference/consent treatment.
- [ ] Ensure promotional messages respect consent and customer preferences.
- [ ] Validate PE ID/header/content ID routing.
- [ ] Maintain DLT template approval and version records.
- [ ] Review provider contract and delivery logs.

**Evidence:** PE registration, headers, approved templates, consent template, SMS provider contract and delivery test.

## I. IT Act / intermediary — lawyer / trust & safety

- [ ] Confirm intermediary status for listings, reviews, chat, posts and comments.
- [ ] Confirm section 79 safe-harbour eligibility and due diligence.
- [ ] Publish platform rules, privacy policy, user agreement and prohibited-content list.
- [ ] Appoint and publish grievance officer.
- [ ] Implement Rule 3 general complaint timelines: 24-hour acknowledgement and 15-day resolution where applicable.
- [ ] Implement special complaint response timelines for specified intimate imagery/impersonation content.
- [ ] Implement lawful government/court order intake and response.
- [ ] Implement copyright/trademark complaint and takedown process.
- [ ] Implement repeat-infringer tracking.
- [ ] Determine whether any significant social media intermediary or other additional regime applies at future scale.
- [ ] Review community moderation and chat retention policy.

**Evidence:** Legal memo, published policy pages, grievance SOP, takedown SOP, case logs and audit report.

## J. Copyright and book rights — copyright lawyer

- [ ] Obtain written opinion on paid commercial rental of lawfully acquired physical books.
- [ ] Review Copyright Act section 14(a) and the difference from explicit commercial rental rights in section 14(b), (d), (e).
- [ ] Review exhaustion/first-sale doctrine and applicable case law.
- [ ] Review whether any library lending licence or publisher agreement is needed.
- [ ] Prohibit pirated photocopies, counterfeit books and unauthorised reproduction.
- [ ] Review use of cover images, metadata and user-uploaded photos.
- [ ] Create notice-and-takedown and repeat-infringer workflow.
- [ ] Review owner declaration and indemnity clauses.

**Evidence:** Written copyright opinion, owner declaration, content/IP policy and takedown SOP.

## K. State/local operating registrations — CS / local counsel

- [ ] Confirm Telangana Shops and Establishments Act registration for any covered Hyderabad office/establishment.
- [ ] Confirm Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments Act registration for any covered Bengaluru office/establishment.
- [ ] Confirm municipal trade licence and local premises approvals if applicable.
- [ ] Confirm fire/occupancy/building-use approvals for any operational premises.
- [ ] Confirm professional tax, EPFO, ESI, POSH and other labour obligations based on employee/contractor facts.
- [ ] Run NSWS Know Your Approvals for actual activity and states; verify all outputs with state departments.
- [ ] Confirm public handover-point contracts, permissions, hours and venue safety.

**Evidence:** State registration certificates, local permits or written N/A opinion, NSWS KYA record, venue agreements.

## L. Trademark and IP — trademark agent / CS

- [ ] Run exact and phonetic ShelfShare search in IP India database.
- [ ] Search candidate Classes 35, 41, 42 and 9; confirm correct classes with agent.
- [ ] Search MCA company name, domains, social handles and app stores.
- [ ] Obtain written clearance opinion.
- [ ] Decide word mark and logo filing strategy.
- [ ] File TM-A if approved.
- [ ] Track examination, opposition, renewal and use evidence.

**Evidence:** Search report, clearance opinion, filing acknowledgement and docket.

## M. Launch go/no-go

- [ ] All P0 legal decisions resolved.
- [ ] Payment provider confirms marketplace and deposit structure in writing.
- [ ] CA approves GST and income-tax treatment.
- [ ] CS confirms entity and state registrations.
- [ ] Lawyer approves consumer, marketplace, privacy, intermediary and copyright policies.
- [ ] Grievance and support operations are staffed.
- [ ] Public handover points are validated.
